Before you add passwords or ID numbers

This is not an encrypted password vault. Your rules, including their values, are stored unencrypted in this Firefox profile's extension storage (browser.storage.local). Anyone or anything that can read your Firefox profile folder, such as someone using your computer account, malware, or a backup of the profile, can read them. Firefox's Primary Password does not protect them. They are never synced, and this add-on never sends them anywhere.

Site access. The add-on only runs on the sites listed below. Firefox asks you once per site when you save its first rule, and it records which sites you allowed (not your values) in its own profile settings. Deleting a site's last rule removes that access. In about:addons, leave this add-on's "Access your data for all websites" switch off.
